Stay Pain-Free
How to avoid your back pain coming back after recovery
🛡
Most back pain returns because of habits, not injury. The good news: it's almost entirely preventable. Here's what Dr. Kapoor recommends for desk workers and gym users like you.
What usually causes flare-ups
1
Long unbroken sitting Sitting for 2+ hours without moving puts 3× your body weight on your lower disc. Set a 90-min movement reminder.
2
Stopping your core exercises after "feeling better" Pain going away doesn't mean you're healed — it means the exercises are working. Keep doing them 3× per week even when pain-free.
3
Sudden return to heavy lifting The most common gym injury. Always get Dr. Kapoor's clearance before returning to deadlifts, squats, or overhead presses.
4
High stress periods Stress causes muscle guarding that loads your spine. Keep the morning mobility routine especially during stressful weeks.
5
Poor sleep position Sleeping on your stomach strains your lower back. Side-sleeping with a pillow between your knees is ideal for your condition.
Your long-term maintenance plan (after Week 24)
✓
3× core exercises per week (for life) 15 minutes, 3 days a week keeps your back supported. This is your insurance policy.
✓
Movement breaks every 90 minutes at your desk Just stand, walk for 2 minutes. Your lower back will thank you.
✓
Annual check-in with Dr. Kapoor Even when pain-free. Catches any early deterioration before it becomes a problem.
